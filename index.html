Frequently Asked Questions

How does CCMG handle local compliance for foreign investors?

We leverage deep understanding of the local regulatory environment to navigate permits, environmental clearances, and legal requirements efficiently. We act as your on-ground compliance officer, coordinating with relevant authorities.

What ESG standards do you align with?

Our frameworks align with GRI, SASB, ISO 14001, and the UN SDGs — ensuring your projects meet international investment criteria and donor requirements.

Can you represent us in negotiations with government bodies?

Yes, CCMG specializes in government liaison and advocacy. We act as your authorized representative to facilitate transparent, effective, and compliant dialogue with public sector entities.

What is your "integrator model"?

Unlike traditional consultancies that only advise, we integrate into your team. We take ownership of outcomes, coordinating between technical, legal, and financial stakeholders to ensure project delivery.

Do you help with Carbon Credit projects?

Absolutely. We assist in identification, validation, and verification of carbon projects under VCS and Gold Standard methodologies, helping clients monetize their environmental impact.
