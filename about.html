Our Story

19 Years of Delivering in Sri Lanka's Most Complex Environments

A legacy firm redefined. Institutional depth meets modern advisory — now rebuilt for the next chapter of Sri Lanka's development story.

The Company

Built on relationships.
Rebuilt for impact.

CCMG was founded on a premise that still holds: that Sri Lanka's most consequential projects — the ones that move capital, reshape infrastructure, and shift environmental trajectories — are won not through RFP responses alone, but through trust built over years inside its corridors of power.

For nearly two decades, the firm has served as the indispensable local partner for foreign investors, development banks, and government agencies navigating the country's regulatory and institutional complexity. From the post-tsunami reconstruction effort (TAARP) to the $675M Mahaweli Water Security Investment Program with the ADB, CCMG has been at the table when it mattered most.

Today, under renewed leadership, the firm is expanding into Sri Lanka's next high-value frontier: sustainability and ESG advisory, carbon market development, and the PPP pipeline that will define the country's infrastructure decade. The same institutional access. A sharper, more modern advisory capability.

Track Record

Two decades.
Defining moments.

From post-conflict rebuilding to modern sustainability infrastructure, CCMG's history is measured in landmark engagements.

05
2005 Post-Tsunami Recovery

TAARP — Tsunami-Affected Area Rebuilding Programme

CCMG contributes to Sri Lanka's largest post-disaster reconstruction effort, coordinating donor-funded infrastructure and housing programs across the affected coastline.

12
2012 U.S. Embassy

U.S. Embassy Facility Advisory

Strategic local representation and liaison support for a major U.S. government facility project in Colombo, demonstrating CCMG's trusted position in high-security, high-compliance engagements.

18
2018 ADB / Water

Mahaweli Water Security Investment Program — $675M

Advisory engagement on Sri Lanka's landmark ADB-backed water transfer and irrigation modernisation program. 350,000+ beneficiaries. A defining moment in CCMG's donor-funded infrastructure capability.

22
2022 Mallavi Urban

Mallavi Urban Water Supply Project

Urban water infrastructure advisory for one of Sri Lanka's Northern Province reconstruction programs — expanding the firm's portfolio into post-conflict infrastructure development.
